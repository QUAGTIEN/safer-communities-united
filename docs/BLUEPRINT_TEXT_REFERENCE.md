1. WEBSITE GOALS
The Safer Communities United website aims to:
 • Show residents that the current Community Plan is causing problems and consequences for the
   health, safety, environment, and welfare of Keystone–Odessa.
 • Clearly present the issues related to roads, water and septic, infrastructure, growth, housing, and
   access to essential services.
 • Demonstrate these issues through data, maps, public records, official documents, and the actual
   conditions of the community.
 • Show the connection between Community Plan rules → actual conditions → consequences for
   people and the environment.
 • Compare the Keystone–Odessa Community Plan with the Hillsborough County Comprehensive Plan
   to clearly show the differences in policies, planning tools, and the ability to address current issues.
 • Present the proposal to remove the current Community Plan and move to an appropriate countywide
   planning framework.

official records. Each section should focus on only one main message.
