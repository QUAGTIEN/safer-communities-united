Two planning frameworks. Two approaches to roads and safety.

Rural road characteristics
Should a rural appearance come before people’s daily needs? The plan prioritizes two-lane rural roads and limits changes that increase capacity or urbanize the area. (Community Plan, Transportation)
Road configuration
Why limit the options before assessing what people need? The plan identifies preferred road configurations, including two-lane rural sections, and the types of improvements considered appropriate for the area.
Gunn Highway

Lighting
Does someone have to get hurt first?
The plan allows lighting when public safety is at risk.
Identify the danger. Act before someone is injured.
Transportation improvements
Should a safety improvement have to pass a rural-character test? The plan allows intersection, cycling, walking and traffic-control improvements while requiring rural character to be maintained.
HILLSBOROUGH COUNTY COMPREHENSIVE PLAN
Transportation safety
Includes countywide policies to improve transportation safety, reduce crashes, and address safety for all users, including drivers, pedestrians and cyclists. (Mobility Section)
Mobility
