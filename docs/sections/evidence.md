- **Loại:** later page (TXT:2018-2096). Ảnh mới được duyệt: R3, E1.
- **Route:** nhãn menu **The Facts** mở Evidence & Records; inventory ghi `/evidence` (D-008). Mục active trên header là **The Facts** (gạch chân đỏ, TXT:2065).
- **Thứ tự:** 1. Explore Evidence by Issue (R3) → 2. Evidence Library (E1, gồm khối From Evidence to Action) → Footer.

<!-- TXT:2019-2030 -->
```text
1. PAGE PURPOSE


Evidence & Records is the evidence center of the entire website, bringing together the data, maps, public records, Community Plan, County policies, and official documents used throughout the issue and comparison sections.
This page must help visitors:
* Check the facts behind the issues presented on the website.
* View the data, maps, and public records directly.
* Understand what each source proves.
* Go from a finding on the website to the original document.


Evidence & Records does not analyze Roads, Water, or Growth again. Roads & Safety, Water & Septic, and Growth, Infrastructure & Daily Life already do that. Evidence & Records serves as the evidence hub.
```

Hiện có URL công khai cho thẻ 1 (Livable Communities); các thẻ còn lại chưa có (OQ-22, OQ-23).
