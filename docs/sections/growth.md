PAGE 05 — GROWTH, INFRASTRUCTURE & DAILY LIFE
This is a very important page because it shows that the Community Plan does not only affect roads and water; the rules about density, land, and development also directly affect housing, distance to services, travel needs, and the ability of infrastructure to keep up with the community.
The Master Assessment clearly identified this chain: a low-density pattern makes housing more spread out → services are farther away → greater dependence on cars → longer trips → more traffic pressure.
